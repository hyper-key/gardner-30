This development is incomplete for the requested end-to-end theorem m3(30)=31.

The kernel-checked structural results now start from the unchanged Good 30 q
predicate and q.length=30. They establish:

* No isolated queens, using the equality case of the perimeter argument.
* The coarse saturation restrictions, then fifteen paired rows and columns.
* Preservation of literal legality and maximality under transposition and
  reflection; an orientation with a complete difference family is available.
* A diagonal cover of the unused 15-by-15 product, requiring 28 to 30 diagonals.
* Equal unused spans when there are fewer than thirty diagonals.
* Equal centered axis sums when the oriented sum family is deficient.
* The H=21A^2-5A2 identity for the fully paired case, derived from the actual
  affine line cover, including the complete two-constant-factor expansion.

Reduction.lean packages these deductions in NecessaryProfile. Its
lower31_of_profile_exclusion theorem has an explicit unproved argument
excluding all such profiles. This is a conditional interface, not the requested
unconditional lower bound. No theorem asserting that argument is supplied.

The residual moment part has also been replaced with kernel-checkable proof
certificates. MomentCuts.lean proves soundness of linear/quadratic bounds and
exhaustive splits over the allowed residual values. ResidualMoments.lean proves
that removing two copies of each required saturated key yields a model of that
checker, allowing further saturations and arbitrary residual multiplicities.
PairedResiduals.lean connects this model to actual integer queen coordinates
whose row and column projections are paired.

MomentData.lean provides kernel proofs for all 2,032 original moment leaves,
using decide +kernel. Three original leaves require finite splits; the others
use direct linear or quadratic separators. The memoized generator visits
2,888 distinct cut states and 108 split states. MomentContexts00..15.lean verify each
numerical problem against the corresponding concrete axes and required keys
and transfer its exclusion to the paired coordinate model. The generator is
untrusted: every generated mathematical assertion is checked by Lean.

What remains outside the kernel is substantial:

1. Formalize the ordinary-diagonal-to-modular projection filters, including
   the two separate exception budgets in the fully paired equal-span case,
   and derive the unequal-span endpoint balance from actual assignments.
2. Prove exhaustive generation of all projected domains (9,016 and 11,691
   representatives) and the zero-result unequal-span enumeration. Successful
   execution of the existing programs does not discharge these obligations.
3. Formalize and instantiate the outer local rejection proofs: unsupported
   targets, forced keys, matchings, and cover trees. The moment terminals are
   now proved, but the entire 20,707-record collection is not yet a kernel proof.
4. Discharge the global profile exclusion and combine the unconditional
   lower31 theorem with upper31 and the existing attained-minimum definition.
