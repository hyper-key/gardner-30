# Coordinator’s research ledger

All runs used the existing provisioned box. Wall times and CPU times are labeled where they are used. The search stages were single-worker processes. The coordinator kept the standard coordinate specification and a separate geometric validator. Agent agreement triggered a new check against the data or proof. It was never the acceptance condition. Full configurations, seeds, raw output, and negative fixtures are in the paths listed below.

| Is an unrestricted solve informative at 30? | Z3 seed 0. Fixed-k baselines for 5,6 at 10 s and 7,8,10 at 5 s. | Can a small repair of the 31 witness work? | Delete d, insert d−1, complete shells d=1…4. ## Evidence map

* The formal benchmark, challenge hashes, accepted kernels and intentional rejections are in formal/README.md and formal/logs/. The repo root contains the fixed challenge and witness proof.
* construction/research.txt gives the construction ledger, provenance, seeds, near misses, modular tight-case and periodic studies, and independent domain audit. construction/replay.sh reruns the longer construction experiments.
* exact/README.md documents the full PB encoding, model projection oracle, mutations, repairs, fixed-axis realizers, checked certificate trees, and per-stage timings. exact/replay_frames.sh regenerates both decisive equal-span domains.
* structure/arguments.md proves the original-to-computation implications and exhausts the cases. structure/cover_coefficients.py independently evaluates the grid functional. structure/unequal_axes.cpp and formal/Unequal.cpp take independent routes through the final empty domain.
* validation/check.py is the standalone witness checker. For example, run python3 Gardner/validation/check.py Gardner/construction/witness31.json --expect 31 from the project root.
